Derive the complete W+ W- W3 h h tree boundary for the null Higgs-pair shift
in renormalizable SU(2) with one fundamental doublet and degenerate W masses.
Use all vector legs on shell, physical polarizations and fixed momenta.
Check the diagram count independently, physical residues, boundedness and
the analytic constant against signed Cauchy reconstruction. Include
nonvanishing and vanishing nontrivial polarization controls. Compare
Ke–Liu–Mahbub, Ema et al., Franken–Schwinn and Jin–Feng explicitly.
Do not infer ALT failure, gauge-dependent off-shell fractions, loop results,
or acceptance at a journal. Archive only independently validated evidence.
