# Related work that constrains novelty claims

| Work | Established result | Consequence for this project |
|---|---|---|
| Grassi, Kniehl, Sirlin (2002), DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevD.65.085001 | Nielsen identities establish gauge independence of unstable-particle complex poles and pole-residue definitions to all orders. | Do not claim gauge independence of the complex pole itself as new. |
| Lewandowski (2018), arXiv:1803.11434 | LSZ treatment of mixed gauge fields, complex-pole residue factorization, vector/scalar mixing, and the Z-photon example. | Do not claim first treatment of complex-pole residues with gauge-field mixing. |
| Denner and Pelliccioli (2020), DOI: 10.1007/JHEP09(2020)164 | Gauge-invariant polarized W-pair signals defined using the doubly-resonant part of the amplitude, including NLO QCD. | Pole-based polarization observables already exist; the target must be a more general amplitude-level connection. |
| Del Gratta et al. (2026), arXiv:2609.25217v1 | Standard two-qutrit tomography can fail for H -> ZZ* -> 4l because coherent gamma*/Z* contributions do not admit the assumed universal production-decay map. | This supplies a concrete phenomenological test case for separating a pole spin state from a general off-shell current. |

The novelty target is the *joint construction* of a spin-resolved complex-pole residue and a separate off-shell current/interference description, together with an on-shell-recursive derivation of higher-point current constraints. This remains a research hypothesis, not an established novelty claim.
