# One Higgs radiated by the massive line: cancellation of an inner gauge variation

**Scope.** This is a same-order addition to the [Yukawa-source exchange subset](https://github.com/lm846802297-bit/off-shell-vector-polarization/tree/5ec052181b83d9b43f1ef4999a1b882538376f6b/research-baselines/su2-fivepoint-yukawa-source). It computes only the diagrams in which one external Higgs is radiated from the massive fermion line and one is emitted at the bridge to the distinguishable conserved fermion pair. It includes the W and Goldstone on that bridge, both positions of the Yukawa insertion on the massive line, and both Higgs permutations. It tests the gauge-dependent remainder of the earlier subset, not a complete six-point classification, cross section, or loop result. Ke–Liu–Mahbub [arXiv:2611.13096v1 §4.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.13096v1#S4.SS2) distinguish an off-shell ST identity obtained from the Lagrangian from an on-shell constructibility claim. This is a Lagrangian check.

## Conventions and endpoint identity

Same model as the pinned prior result: the left component of the massive fermion belongs to the lower T³=−1/2 doublet component; the right component is a singlet; the scalar Yukawa coupling is real. Use metric (+---), a=g/2, m=a v, M=m_h, fermion mass μ, and b=μ/v. In the phase Φ_lower=(v+h+iχ)/√2 with Fourier waves exp(−ip·x) the bare vertices are −ia γ^α P_L, −ib for h, and +b γ5 for χ. The other fermion line is massless, distinguishable and conserved. Strip its common fermion coupling, color and stationary propagator factors. Physical signed momenta are all incoming. For the massive line p_f and p_b, let q=p_f+p_b, spinors u and vbar satisfying slash(p_f)u=μu and vbar slash(p_b)=−μvbar, and P=vbar γ5 u. Then q·j=μP, where j^α=vbar γ^α P_L u.

Assign x to the Yukawa line and y to the bridge, so q+x+K+y=0 and Q=q+x. The two internal fermion momenta, flowing from the u spinor to vbar, are A=p_f+x and B=−p_b−x. With S(l)=(slash(l)+μ)/(l²−μ²),

```text
J_h^alpha=vbar[γ^alpha P_L S(A) + S(B) γ^alpha P_L]u,
P_h      =vbar[γ5 S(A) + S(B) γ5]u.
```

The exact ordered W and χ source vertices are respectively −iab J_h and +b² P_h. For the first ordering Q=A+p_b gives Q·J_{h,1}=μP_{h,1}+vbar P_R u. For the other ordering Q=p_f−B gives Q·J_{h,2}=μP_{h,2}−vbar P_L u. In this derivation slash(A)S(A)=1+μS(A) and S(B)slash(B)=1+μS(B), and the end-projector signs are fixed before drawing a conclusion. The two identities imply

```text
Q·J_h = μ P_h + P.
```

The contact-like term P means the *radiative* source does not satisfy the earlier single-vertex Ward identity by itself.

## Cancellation of the targeted gauge variation

At the far end define the bare W vertex as iT, with T=2am e in Lorentz-vector notation, and V=Q·T. The bare Goldstone vertex is G=a(Q−y)·e, giving V=mG by K·e=0. Put D_Q=Q²−m² and C_Q=Q²−ξm². Multiplication of the raw ordered source vertices and the two propagators gives, in the earlier normalization after removing −ia,

```text
A_rad = b [J_h·T − (1−ξ)(Q·J_h)V/C_Q]/D_Q − b μ P_h G/(m C_Q)
      = b [J_h·T − (Q·J_h)V/m²]/D_Q + b P V/(m² C_Q).
```

There is therefore a generally nonzero gauge remnant within this isolated class. Relative to ξ=1 it is exactly

```text
δξ A_rad(x|y) = +2a² (ξ−1)(q·j)(Q·e)/(D_Q C_Q).
```

The prior root-sewn contribution for the matching bridge momentum Q has the **opposite** variation. Their sum vanishes for each x|y assignment and hence after the p↔r sum. The cancellation is between these specified tree sectors; dropping either fermion ordering or the bridge Goldstone alters it. Other source-line sectors, such as two Higgs emissions on the massive line or a Yukawa-produced virtual h splitting through hhh, were not included or used to assert full closure.

## Independent computation and replay

The numerical method constructs actual physical signed Dirac spinor solutions, derives the Yukawa vertices from the action, and multiplies the separate raw ordered matrices and full R_ξ propagators. The far cubic vertices are generated by Fourier coefficient extraction. It compares the ordered source matrices, the two separate endpoint terms, the signed gauge variation of the radiated diagrams, and the targeted older W/χ exchange ordering computed using the pinned validated action helper. It records raw per-order vector and Goldstone contributions for both Higgs assignments. The old direct_amplitudes helper is byte-derived from the pinned prior Yukawa-source script (blob fb8f50a1379175c086a8c7899a358b50378f622a / SHA-256 5cbc4e38401ae04615865b506c4cd4cbbf7ade6845b365ed333bc47684b18605); its reuse is not new independent evidence of that prior sector. The independent new content is the ordered fermion-line amplitude construction and signed interference comparison. Over 20 accepted main and holdout fixtures at ξ=0.43, 1, 2.65, the radiated-class and old-class variations cancel to 1.72e-16 scaled. Removing one near-line ordering or the Goldstone bridge gives nonzero mismatches of 0.188 and 0.0887 respectively in the archived scaling. The symbolic test verifies the Ward-based simplification and exact gauge coefficient; the numeric test is a direct signed amplitude comparison, not a norm-only match. The model parameters and generic physical signed four-vectors are synthetic fixtures. No new public data or reconstructed private history are used.
